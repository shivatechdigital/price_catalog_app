YOUR DATA

Privacy Policy

Information we collect

PriceCatalog collects your name, email address, phone number, business details, city, GST details, and account identifier when you register or update your profile. Traders and administrators may also upload product, business, or document images when using relevant features.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate accounts, manage trader approvals, publish product catalogues, process requirements, send account and workflow notifications, provide support, and keep the service secure.

Firebase and service providers

The app uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for sign-in, data storage, file storage, and notifications. These providers process information to provide these services.

Permissions

Camera and photo library access is requested only when an administrator chooses to capture or select product images. Notifications are optional and can be changed in device Settings.

Data retention

Business requirements and audit records may be retained where necessary for legitimate business, security, legal, or audit purposes. We retain only what is reasonably needed for these purposes.

Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your account inside the app:

  1. Open My Profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your password and the deletion.

Account deletion removes your Firebase Authentication account, profile, notifications, uploaded personal files, trader documents, catalogs, and exports. Some business or audit records may be retained where legally or operationally required.

You must provide accurate account information and use PriceCatalog only for lawful business activities. Keep your login credentials secure and notify the app owner if you suspect unauthorized access.

Administrators manage catalogues and trader approvals. Traders may submit requirements and use approved account features. We may suspend access when an account violates these terms or creates a security risk.

You are responsible for having the right to upload product images, documents, and other content. Do not upload unlawful, deceptive, or harmful material.

Account deletion

You may delete your account from the in-app Delete Account option. Deletion is permanent. Records that must be retained for legal, security, or business audit purposes may remain as described in the Privacy Policy.

Service availability

We work to keep the app and its backend available and accurate, but access may occasionally be affected by maintenance, updates, network problems, or third-party service interruptions.
